Corporate Overview

Company Overview

The purpose of the Bank is to conduct banking and investment activities in compliance with Islamic Sharia for its own account or on behalf of others, locally and internationally.

History

More Than 50 Years of Banking

Al Rajhi Bank’s banking and commercial activities began more than 50 years ago. Its individual establishments were subsequently combined into Al Rajhi Trading and Exchange Corporation and later converted into a Saudi joint-stock company.
